Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I sell my house?
With a cash house buyer like Cash Home, you can sell your house in as little as 7 to 21 days. Traditional estate agent sales typically take 4-6 months.
Can I sell below market value?
Yes, selling to a cash buyer usually involves a discount (typically 15-20% below market value) in exchange for a guaranteed, ultra-fast sale without any estate agent fees or legal costs.
Are cash house buyers legitimate?
Reputable cash house buyers are legitimate. Always check if the company is a member of The Property Ombudsman or the National Association of Property Buyers (NAPB).
